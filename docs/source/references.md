# References

These references cover the exact-diagonalization methods, numerical libraries, and analytic benchmarks used in the documentation. Implementation-specific provenance is recorded in [`PROVENANCE.md`](https://github.com/QuantumArtificer/edinpy/blob/main/PROVENANCE.md).

## Exact diagonalization and Fock-space methods

H. Q. Lin, J. E. Gubernatis, H. Gould, and J. Tobochnik, "Exact Diagonalization Methods for Quantum Systems," *Computers in Physics* **7**, 400-407 (1993). [doi:10.1063/1.4823192](https://doi.org/10.1063/1.4823192)

A. W. Sandvik, "Computational Studies of Quantum Spin Systems," *AIP Conference Proceedings* **1297**, 135-338 (2010). [arXiv:1101.3281](https://arxiv.org/abs/1101.3281)

J. M. Zhang and R. X. Dong, "Exact diagonalization: the Bose-Hubbard model as an example," *European Journal of Physics* **31**, 591-602 (2010). [doi:10.1088/0143-0807/31/3/016](https://doi.org/10.1088/0143-0807/31/3/016), [arXiv:1102.4006](https://arxiv.org/abs/1102.4006)

## Eigensolvers and numerical libraries

R. B. Lehoucq, D. C. Sorensen, and C. Yang, *ARPACK Users' Guide: Solution of Large-Scale Eigenvalue Problems with Implicitly Restarted Arnoldi Methods*, SIAM (1998). [doi:10.1137/1.9780898719628](https://doi.org/10.1137/1.9780898719628)

P. Virtanen *et al.*, "SciPy 1.0: Fundamental Algorithms for Scientific Computing in Python," *Nature Methods* **17**, 261-272 (2020). [doi:10.1038/s41592-019-0686-2](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41592-019-0686-2)

C. R. Harris *et al.*, "Array programming with NumPy," *Nature* **585**, 357-362 (2020). [doi:10.1038/s41586-020-2649-2](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41586-020-2649-2)
